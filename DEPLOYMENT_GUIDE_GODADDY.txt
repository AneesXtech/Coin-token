HOW TO DEPLOY ON GODADDY IN 3 MINUTES:
------------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1: Log in to your GoDaddy Account
   - Go to your "My Products" page.
   - Click "Manage" next to "Web Hosting" (cPanel).
   - Click "cPanel Admin".

STEP 2: Open File Manager
   - In cPanel, find and click "File Manager".
   - Double-click on the "public_html" folder (or your subdomain folder).
   - (Note: If you have old default files like index.html or hostingstart.html, delete them).

STEP 3: Upload & Extract
   - Click "Upload" at the top toolbar.
   - Select and upload "HouseFacts-GoDaddy-Production-Ready.zip".
   - Once the upload bar turns green (100%), go back to File Manager.
   - Right-click on the uploaded ZIP file and click "Extract".
   - Click "Extract Files".

STEP 4: Test Your Website
   - Open your domain in any browser: https://yourdomain.com
   - The homepage, 3D Helix, animations, and Contact page will be live immediately!
   - Test submitting the Contact form or Newsletter form to verify email delivery to Alejom.prs@gmail.com.
